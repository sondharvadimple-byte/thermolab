--- X-08  Test case 6.4  (Test-case PDF 6.4) ---
Scenario: A metal plate initially at 200°C is suddenly exposed to 25°C air. Its temperature changes with time. Analyse transient heat transfer.
Verdict: INSUFFICIENT DATA / MODEL REQUIRED
    - Transient heat transfer needs a time-dependent model (lumped capacitance or transient conduction with a heat-transfer coefficient), which is not implemented. The steady and cyclic models would give a misleading answer.
